# Triangle of Solomon — Narrative Alchemy Notes

## The Artifact

Central implement of Solomonic ceremonial magic (Goetia / Lemegeton). Triangle with inscribed circle. Three sides carry divine names:

- Right: TETRAGRAMMATON
- Left: ANAPHEXETON (also ANACHEXETON or ANEXHEXETON)
- Base: PRIMEMATUM
- Corner letters spell MICHAEL — archangel as guardian presence

The inscribed circle is where the spirit is compelled to appear. The magician stands in a separate magic circle and commands the entity into the Triangle, which acts as containment vessel. The spirit must not leave the Triangle during the operation.

The divine names are technology, not decoration. The geometry does work the will alone cannot do.

Sources: Goetia, Mathers/Crowley ed. (1904); Francis Barrett, The Magus (1801); Greater Key of Solomon (Clavicula Salomonis). Cavendish synthesizes rather than transcribes — readable but edges are smoothed.

---

## The Three Sides — Functional Structure

Ground → Call → Contain

- TETRAGRAMMATON = pure being, the ground
- ANAPHEXETON = the compeller, draws entity toward manifestation
- PRIMEMATUM = the binder, holds it in place once it arrives

---

## 21c / Narrative Alchemy Reading

The Solomonic system as technology for state management and attention direction. All the elaborate preparation (fasting, robes, planetary timing, precise wording) engineers a specific quality of consciousness before the operation begins. By the time the magician steps into the circle, attention is completely saturated. No room for the ordinary mind to interfere.

Chaos magick identified this and stripped the apparatus to its functional minimum. But the container is part of the operation. Stripping it back costs something.

The triangle made of words:

1. Framing question — opens the space
2. Generative prompt — calls something forward (from the unconscious, from text, from the AI collaborator)
3. Constraint — gives it a form to inhabit; without constraint it diffuses back into possibility

Mapped to existing practice:

- Daily journal = the circle (protected space the magician stands in)
- The question brought to the page = the triangle
- Names are interchangeable by chaos magick logic (Wittgenstein / Hillman / north star seed phrase)
- The entity in the middle = the essay, the fragment
- Production pipeline = the circle that protects from being consumed (Capture → Shape → Release)

---

## Closing the Operation

The Solomonic texts are serious about this: close properly. The spirit gets licence to depart. Don't leave the triangle open.

21c equivalent: publication. Fragment goes out. Operation closes. Step back into ordinary time. You don't live inside the evocation. You do the operation and step back out of the circle.
